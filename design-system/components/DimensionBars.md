# DimensionBars

The six diagnosis dimensions — Analysis, Planning, Execution, Risk, Discipline, Emotion — always in that order, each with its `dim-*` color.

- Structure: `tt-dims` > `tt-dim` rows (swatch + label · `tt-dim__bar` > `<i class="dim-…">` · value). The primary problem dimension (highest cost R, F-15 AC3) gets `tt-dim--primary` (bold) and a "มิติหลัก" badge nearby.
- Values: mistake cost in R (or $), quality score, or share of mistakes — say which in the card title.
- `dim-*` colors are for marks only; labels stay `ink`.
